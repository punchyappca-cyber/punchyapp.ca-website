Beyond a Punch Clock

Attendance accountability,
not just a timestamp

Punchy compares every clock-in and clock-out against the actual scheduled shift, so early arrivals, late arrivals, early departures, and late departures are surfaced automatically instead of buried in a spreadsheet nobody reads until payroll day.

  • Forgot to clock out? Staff report the real time themselves - reviewed and approved by Admin, Manager, or Accounting, never silently guessed
  • Per-employee reliability scoring and a full attendance report per location, with a personal-only view for staff (no leaderboard)
  • An Open Shifts dashboard for every unclosed clock-in, company-wide, with a manual force-close option for edge cases

Leave & Remote Work

Leave and WFH requests,
without the group chat

Staff apply for leave or work-from-home right in the app. Every request is automatically checked against existing shifts and other approved time off before a Manager ever sees it, so nobody ends up accidentally double-booked or short-staffed on the same day.

  • Automatic conflict detection against shifts and other approved leave/WFH
  • One-tap Approve/Reject for Managers, with instant success feedback and a notification sent to staff
  • Optional read-only visibility for Accounting, and WFH itself can be switched off per location that doesn't offer remote work

From Clock to Paycheque

Payroll that starts
with real clock data

Nobody re-types hours into a spreadsheet. Every clock-in, break, and clock-out already recorded in Punchy feeds straight into a two-person review chain before a single paystub is generated.

  • A Manager reviews and submits their team's pay period; Accounting cross-checks it before approving
  • Canadian statutory deductions and statutory holiday pay calculated automatically per location
  • Approval generates a permanent paystub snapshot per employee - a fixed record, not a document that can silently drift later
