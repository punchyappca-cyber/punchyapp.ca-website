Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 8, 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains what information Punchy ("we", "us", "our") collects through the Punchy app and website, how we use it, and the choices you have. It applies to Admins, Managers, Accounting staff, and Employees using the Service.

2. Information We Collect

• Account information: name, email address, phone number, date of birth (used for payroll identity verification), and role within your company. • Work data: work location assignments, schedules, clock-in/clock-out and break times, leave and work-from-home requests, and payroll calculations. • Payroll identifiers: where enabled, sensitive identifiers such as a Social Insurance Number are encrypted before storage and are only ever decrypted for authorized Admin/Accounting users of your own company. • Profile photo: an optional photo you upload, compressed and stored securely. • Device permissions: camera access (to scan QR codes for clock-in/out), photo library access (to choose a profile photo), and Face ID/biometric access (to unlock the app quickly) - biometric verification happens entirely on your device and is never sent to us.

3. How We Use Information

We use the information collected to operate the scheduling, attendance, leave, and payroll features of the Service, to communicate with you about your account and requests (e.g. leave approvals, missed clock-out reviews), to maintain the security and integrity of the Service, and to provide customer support.

4. Who Can See Your Information

Information you enter is visible only within your own company: your assigned Admin, Managers, and Accounting staff can see the work and payroll data relevant to their role, and other employees at your company can see basic directory information (such as your name and photo) where the app displays staff lists. We do not sell your information or share it with unrelated third parties.

5. Third-Party Service Providers

We use Google Firebase for authentication, data storage, and hosting. These providers process data on our behalf under their own security and privacy commitments, and do not use your data for their own purposes.

6. Data Retention

We retain account and work data for as long as your account is active, and for a reasonable period afterward as needed for payroll, tax, and legal record-keeping purposes, unless you request earlier deletion as described below.

7. Your Rights and Account Deletion

You can review and update most of your information directly in the app under Settings. You can also request deletion of your account and associated personal data at any time from Settings → Delete My Account. We process deletion requests as soon as reasonably possible, subject to any data we are legally required to retain (such as payroll records).

8. Children's Privacy

The Service is intended for use by working adults and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of the App after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

10. Contact Us

Questions about this Privacy Policy or your data can be sent to support@punchyapp.ca.
