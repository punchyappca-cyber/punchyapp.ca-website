Invited by an Admin

Cross-check payroll
before it's final

Accounting isn't a self-service signup — an Admin invites accounting staff and grants exactly the permissions they need, scoped to specific company locations. Their job: verify the numbers before anyone gets paid.

Invited Access, Not a Role Toggle

Every accounting user is tied to the Admin who invited them, with individual permission flags rather than one all-or-nothing switch.

1

Company-scoped

Access is limited to the specific companies/locations an Admin grants — never the whole platform.

2

Permission by permission

Approve payroll, manage staff, view leave/WFH — each is its own toggle, so an Admin can grant exactly as much access as the role needs.

The Payroll Review Chain

Payroll never goes straight from a Manager's submission to a paycheck — Accounting is the verification step in between.

1

Cross-check submitted periods

Review what a Manager submitted against actual clock data before approving — amendments are tracked separately from the original approval.

2

Generate paystubs

Once approved, a paystub snapshot is generated per employee for that pay period — a permanent record, not a live document that can drift.

Handling Sensitive Data

Payroll processing sometimes requires identifiers you can't leave in plain text.

1

Encrypted payroll identifiers

A Social Insurance Number is encrypted before it's ever stored — only authorized Accounting and Admin users can decrypt it, and only for payroll processing.

2

Vacation pay & payouts

Whether a location pays vacation pay out each cheque or banks it, Accounting handles the payout requests for banked balances.

Also Included

What else Accounting can access

Open Shifts

Full visibility into open shifts and missed clock-out reviews across every location they're scoped to — unconditionally, no extra permission flag needed.

Location Details

A read-only view of each location's Admin and Manager contact info, hours, and closures.

Leave & WFH (Optional)

Where an Admin grants it, a read-only combined view of every leave and WFH request across their scoped locations.
