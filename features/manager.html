Scoped to Your Locations

Everything for your location,
none of the noise

A Manager only sees the locations an Admin has assigned them to - never the whole company. Inside that scope, they run the day-to-day: schedules, approvals, and payroll for their own team.

Today, At a Glance

The Manager dashboard opens on what's happening right now, not a menu of settings.

1

Live shift status

Every shift today is shown with its real status - Completed, Clocked In, Not Clocked In, or Upcoming - color-coded, with the location name shown whenever a Manager covers more than one site.

2

Build & copy schedules

Create shifts for your assigned locations and copy an entire pay period forward, with the same print-to-PDF option Admins have for physical posting.

Approvals, One Tap

Leave, work-from-home, and missed clock-out requests all land in one place, with immediate feedback once you act on them.

1

Leave & WFH

Every request is pre-checked for conflicts against shifts and other approved time off - Approve or Reject with one tap, with a confirmation snackbar and an instant notification sent to the employee.

2

Missed clock-out review

When your Admin grants the permission, review a staff member's self-reported "I actually left at..." submission and approve or reject it - nothing gets silently marked as clocked out on their behalf.

Payroll, Ready for Review

Once a pay period closes, submit your team's hours to Accounting instead of exporting a spreadsheet by hand.

1

Submit for cross-check

Review calculated pay per employee, then submit the whole period to Accounting for approval - with permission-gated shift-approval as a step before submission, and clear metric tiles for the totals.

2

Period navigation

Step back and forward between pay periods to review history without waiting on a report export.

What else a Manager can see

Staff Directory (Scoped)

Every employee at your assigned locations - contact info, status, and quick access to their schedule and payroll history.

Location Details

Contact info for the Admin and Accounting person tied to each of your locations, plus attendance and payroll report access.

Open Shifts

See every forgotten clock-out across your locations and either force-close it or wait on the employee's own submission.

Attendance Insight

The same reliability scoring and incident feed Admins see, scoped to just the employees and locations you manage.

Notifications

Recent leave and WFH activity surfaces right on your dashboard, not just buried in a notification list.

No Distractions

No billing, no company-wide settings, no access to locations outside your assignment - the dashboard only shows what's relevant to you.
