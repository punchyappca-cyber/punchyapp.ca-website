Scoped to Your Locations

Everything for your location,
none of the noise

A Manager only sees the locations an Admin has assigned them to — never the whole company. Inside that scope, they run the day-to-day: schedules, approvals, and payroll for their own team.

Scheduling for Your Team

Build and adjust shifts for whichever locations you're assigned to, with an at-a-glance view of who's working today.

1

Today's Shifts, front and center

The Manager dashboard leads with today's activity — who's clocked in, who's upcoming, and how many shifts are scheduled across your locations.

2

Copy schedules forward

Reuse last period's schedule instead of rebuilding it, with the same print-to-PDF option Admins have for physical posting.

Approvals, One Tap

Leave, work-from-home, and missed clock-out requests all land in one place, with immediate feedback once you act on them.

1

Leave & WFH

Every request is pre-checked for conflicts against shifts and other approved time off — Approve or Reject with one tap, and the employee is notified instantly.

2

Missed clock-out review

When permitted, review a staff member's self-reported "I actually left at..." submission and approve or reject it — nothing gets silently marked as clocked out on their behalf.

Payroll, Ready for Review

Once a pay period closes, submit your team's hours to Accounting instead of exporting a spreadsheet by hand.

1

Submit for cross-check

Review calculated pay per employee, then submit the whole period to Accounting for approval — with permission-gated shift-approval as a step before submission.

2

Attendance insight for your team

The same reliability scoring and incident feed Admins see, scoped to just the employees and locations you manage.

Staff Directory (Scoped)

Every employee at your assigned locations — contact info, status, and quick access to their schedule and payroll history.

Location Details

Contact info for the Admin and Accounting person tied to each of your locations, plus attendance and payroll report access.

Open Shifts
