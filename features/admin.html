For Company Owners

Run your entire company
from one dashboard

The Admin is the company owner in Punchy - the only role that self-registers to create an account. Everything else, from locations to staff to payroll, is set up and overseen from here.

Locations & Company Setup

Add every business location your company operates, each with its own working hours, holiday closures, and payroll rules.

1

Per-location configuration

Set working hours by day of week, whether break tracking is required, the province for tax rules, and whether vacation pay is paid out each cheque or banked for later payout.

2

Statutory & manual closures

Mark statutory holidays and one-off administrative closures per location - staff, schedules, and statutory holiday pay all reflect them automatically.

3

Company profile details

Address, phone, fax, employer/business number, and a benefits catalog you can attach to employees - the details Accounting needs at tax time.

Staff, Managers & Accounting

Invite employees directly, or bring on Managers and Accounting staff scoped to only the locations they should see.

1

Full staff directory

Every employee, with photo, contact info, employment type, pay rate, and live status - active, invited, disabled, or deleted.

2

Dedicated Manager & Accounting directories

Separate screens for managing who has Manager or Accounting access, which locations they're assigned to, and which permissions are switched on for each of them individually.

3

Audit trail

Every profile edit, permission change, and forced clock-out is logged, so there's always a record of who changed what and when.

Scheduling, Two Ways

Build shifts directly in the app, or digitize a schedule you already run on paper.

1

Build & copy schedules

Create shifts per location and copy an entire pay period forward instead of rebuilding it from scratch, with a confirmation review before anything overwrites. Print a blank or filled A4 schedule straight to PDF for physical posting.

2

Photo-scan a paper schedule

Still run the schedule on a handwritten sheet? Take a photo - on-device text recognition reads it into digital shift entries for you to review, edit, and confirm before publishing. Nothing is uploaded to the cloud to do this.

Kiosk & Device Control

Every shared clock-in tablet is registered to your account, and stays under your control remotely.

1

Pair with a QR scan

Register a new kiosk device to a specific location in seconds - no manual configuration on the tablet itself.

2

Remote enable/disable

Lost a tablet, or retiring one? Disable it remotely without touching the physical device, or switch your own device between Kiosk Mode and the full Admin app without signing out.

Also Included

Oversight across the whole company

Reports & Insights

Attendance reliability scoring, incident feeds for late/early arrivals and departures, and a side-by-side comparison across every location.

Billing & Plans

Manage your subscription tier and billing from your web dashboard - never an in-app purchase flow to maintain, and never blocking a build submission on payment processing rules.

Security

Sensitive payroll identifiers like a SIN are encrypted before storage, and Face ID/biometric unlock keeps the app itself locked down on-device.

Open Shifts & Missed Clock-Outs

Review every self-reported missed clock-out across the company and approve or reject it, or force-close a shift directly when needed.

Notifications

An in-app notification center keeps you posted on leave/WFH requests, missed clock-out submissions, and payroll events as they happen.

Account Controls

Terms of Service and Privacy Policy built into Settings, plus a self-service account deletion request if you ever need to close things down.
