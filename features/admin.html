For Company Owners

Run your entire company
from one dashboard

The Admin is the company owner in Punchy — the only role that self-registers to create an account. Everything else, from locations to staff to payroll, is set up and overseen from here.

Locations & Company Setup

Add every business location your company operates, each with its own working hours, holiday closures, and payroll rules.

1

Per-location configuration

Set working hours, whether break tracking is required, the province/tax region, and whether vacation pay is paid out each cheque or banked for later payout.

2

Statutory & manual closures

Mark statutory holidays and one-off administrative closures per location — staff and schedules automatically reflect them.

Staff, Managers & Accounting

Invite employees directly, or bring on Managers and Accounting staff scoped to only the locations they should see.

1

Full staff directory

Every employee, with photo, contact info, employment type, pay rate, and live status — active, invited, or disabled.

2

Granular permissions

Managers and Accounting are invited with individual permission toggles — manage staff, approve payroll, view leave/WFH — not an all-or-nothing role.

3

Audit trail

Every profile edit, permission change, and forced clock-out is logged, so there's always a record of who changed what.

Scheduling, Two Ways

Build shifts directly in the app, or digitize a schedule you already run on paper.

1

Build & copy schedules

Create shifts per location and copy an entire pay period forward instead of rebuilding it from scratch. Print a blank or filled A4 schedule straight to PDF.

2

Photo-scan a paper schedule

Still run the schedule on a handwritten sheet? Take a photo — on-device text recognition reads it into digital shift entries for you to review and confirm before publishing. Nothing is uploaded to the cloud to do this.

Also Included

Oversight across the whole company

Reports & Insights

Attendance reliability scoring, incident feeds for late/early arrivals and departures, and a side-by-side comparison across every location.

Billing & Plans

Manage your subscription tier and billing from your web dashboard — never an in-app purchase flow to maintain.

Security

Sensitive payroll identifiers like a SIN are encrypted before storage, and Face ID/biometric unlock keeps the app itself locked down.
