Shared-Device Clock-In

Turn a tablet at the door
into a clock-in terminal

No badge readers, no per-employee hardware. One tablet, mounted anywhere staff walk in, running the same Punchy app in a locked-down Kiosk Mode.

Set Up Once

Pairing a device takes a scan, not an IT ticket.

1

QR-paired to your company

An Admin pairs a device to a specific company and location by scanning a QR code — the device stays registered to that Admin's account from then on.

2

Switch modes without signing out

The same iPad can flip between Kiosk Mode and the full Admin app without ever logging out — useful for a manager's own tablet doing double duty.

The Clock-In Flow

Staff identify themselves at the kiosk, not the other way around.

1

Scan & go

Employees scan their own phone's QR code at the kiosk to clock in, start or end a break, and clock out — no shared PIN pad to remember.

2

Catches a forgotten clock-out

If a shift has drifted well past its scheduled end, the kiosk stops offering a plain clock-out and instead asks the employee to report the real time they left — for review, not a silent guess.

Also Included

Remote control, from anywhere

Enable / Disable Remotely

An Admin can re-authorize or disable a paired kiosk device at any time, without touching the physical tablet.

No Break-Tracking? No Problem

For locations that don't require break tracking, the kiosk skips straight to clock-in/out — one scan, done.

Instant Confirmation

Every action ends with a clear on-screen confirmation, so staff always know their punch went through.
