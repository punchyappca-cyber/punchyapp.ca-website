The Clock-In Flow

Staff identify themselves at the kiosk, not the other way around.

1

Scan & go

Employees scan their own phone's QR code at the kiosk to clock in, start or end a break, and clock out - no shared PIN pad to remember, and a clear on-screen confirmation after every action.

2

Catches a forgotten clock-out

If a shift has drifted well past its scheduled end, the kiosk stops offering a plain clock-out and instead asks the employee to report the real time they left - for review, not a silent guess.

3

Skips what isn't needed

For locations that don't require break tracking, the kiosk skips straight to clock-in/out - one scan, done, no extra steps to confuse staff.

Also Included

Remote control, from anywhere

Enable / Disable Remotely

An Admin can re-authorize or disable a paired kiosk device at any time, without touching the physical tablet.

Auto-Return to Idle

An inactivity timer brings the kiosk back to its idle screen automatically, so it's always ready for the next person and never left mid-flow.

Runs Under the Admin's Session

Kiosk devices operate under the Admin's own account rather than any individual employee's, so a lost or stolen tablet doesn't expose personal staff logins.
