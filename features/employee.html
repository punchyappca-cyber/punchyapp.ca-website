For Every Employee

Your schedule, your hours,
your paystubs — in your pocket

Employees don't sign themselves up — they're invited by an Admin. From there, everything about their work day lives in one app instead of a shared spreadsheet and a paper time sheet.

Clocking In, Your Way

No dedicated hardware to buy per employee — clock in with what's already at the door, or from your own phone.

1

Scan the kiosk QR code

A shared tablet at the entrance displays a QR code — scan it with your phone to clock in or out, start a break, or end one.

2

Face ID app unlock

Log back into the app quickly and securely with Face ID or fingerprint, instead of retyping a password every time.

Forgot to Clock Out?

It happens. Punchy doesn't silently guess a time for you or leave it stuck open forever.

1

Report it yourself

The app detects a shift that's drifted well past its scheduled end and asks you to enter the time you actually left, with an optional note.

2

Reviewed, not assumed

Your submission goes to an Admin, Manager, or Accounting for approval — you're notified the moment it's decided.

Leave, WFH & Pay

Apply for time off and check your pay without a single email to HR.

1

Apply in seconds

Request leave or a work-from-home day; Punchy checks it against your schedule and other approved time off before your Manager ever sees it.

2

Paystubs & personal attendance

View past paystubs, and see your own reliability score and early/late/missed counts — a personal view, never a leaderboard against coworkers.
