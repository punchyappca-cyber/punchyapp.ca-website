Forgot to Clock Out?

It happens. Punchy doesn't silently guess a time for you or leave it stuck open forever.

1

Report it yourself

The app detects a shift that's drifted well past its scheduled end and asks you to enter the time you actually left, with an optional note for context.

2

Reviewed, not assumed

Your submission goes to an Admin, Manager, or Accounting for approval - you're notified the moment it's decided, whether approved or sent back.

Leave, WFH & Pay

Apply for time off and check your pay without a single email to HR.

1

Apply in seconds

Request leave or a work-from-home day; Punchy checks it against your schedule and other approved time off before your Manager ever sees it.

2

Paystubs & personal attendance

View past paystubs, and see your own reliability score and early/late/missed counts - a personal view, never a leaderboard against coworkers.

3

Upcoming pay estimate

See an estimate of your current pay period's hours and gross pay before it's even submitted for approval.
