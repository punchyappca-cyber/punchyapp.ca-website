From the Clock to the Paystub

Payroll built on
real clock data

No re-entering hours into a separate spreadsheet. Every clock-in, break, and clock-out already recorded in Punchy feeds directly into payroll, with a review step before anything is final.

Calculated Automatically

Set it up once per employee, and the math happens on its own every pay period.

1

Per-employee, per-location rates

Set an hourly rate and employment type per employee — with rate overrides per location for staff who work more than one site.

2

Statutory holiday pay

When a location marks a day as a statutory holiday, eligible staff are paid for it automatically, calculated per your configured rule.

3

Vacation pay, your way

Paid out on every cheque, or banked and paid out to an employee on request — set per location.

A Real Review Chain

Nobody's pay goes out on one person's say-so.

1

Manager submits the period

Once a pay period closes, a Manager reviews the calculated hours and pay for their team, then submits it for approval.

2

Accounting cross-checks & approves

Accounting verifies the submission against clock data before approving — and can still make a tracked amendment afterward if something needs correcting.

3

Paystub, generated

Approval generates a permanent paystub snapshot per employee for that period — a fixed record, not a live document.

Also Included

Keeping payroll data safe

Encrypted Payroll IDs

A Social Insurance Number is encrypted before it's ever stored, decrypted only for authorized payroll processing.

Billing, Handled Separately

Your Punchy subscription itself is managed through your web dashboard — never an in-app purchase to reconcile.

Exportable Reports

Generate payroll and attendance reports per location, ready to hand to a bookkeeper or accountant outside Punchy.
