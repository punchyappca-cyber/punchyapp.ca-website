Multi-Location From Day One

Every location,
its own rules

A downtown café and a warehouse across town don't run on the same hours or the same payroll policy. Punchy treats every business location as its own configuration, under one company.

Per-Location Configuration

Each location carries its own operating details, set once by the Admin.

1

Hours & break policy

Working hours and whether break tracking is required are set per location, not forced company-wide.

2

Holiday closures

Mark statutory holidays and one-off administrative closures — schedules and statutory holiday pay reflect them automatically.

3

Vacation pay method

Choose whether vacation pay is added to every paycheque, or banked and paid out to staff on request.

Scoped Access, By Design

Nobody sees more of the company than their role needs to.

1

Admin sees everything

Every location, every employee, every payroll period — the full picture, always.

2

Managers & Accounting see their assignment

A Manager assigned to two locations never sees a third they weren't given access to — same for invited Accounting staff.

Also Included

Comparing performance across sites

Location Comparison

Admins can compare attendance reliability and labor metrics side-by-side across every location.

Location Detail View

A tappable card showing the Admin, Manager, and Accounting contact for that location — reachable by call or directions.

Print-Ready Schedules

Print a blank or filled A4 schedule for a specific location, ready to post physically on-site.
