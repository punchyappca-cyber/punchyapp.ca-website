Multi-Location From Day One

Every location,
its own rules

A downtown cafe and a warehouse across town don't run on the same hours or the same payroll policy. Punchy treats every business location as its own configuration, under one company.

Per-Location Configuration

Each location carries its own operating details, set once by the Admin.

1

Hours & break policy

Working hours are set per day of week, and whether break tracking is required is set per location, not forced company-wide - a warehouse might require it while a small kiosk doesn't.

2

Holiday closures

Mark statutory holidays and one-off administrative closures - schedules and statutory holiday pay reflect them automatically, using a configurable rule (none, percentage, or standard-average).

3

Vacation pay method

Choose whether vacation pay is added to every paycheque, or banked and paid out to staff on request.

Scoped Access, By Design

Nobody sees more of the company than their role needs to.

1

Admin sees everything

Every location, every employee, every payroll period - the full picture, always.

2

Managers & Accounting see their assignment

A Manager assigned to two locations never sees a third they weren't given access to - same for invited Accounting staff, enforced the same way at the data layer, not just hidden in the UI.

Comparing Performance Across Sites

Once you're running more than one location, comparing them matters.

1

Side-by-side comparison

Admins can compare attendance reliability and labor metrics across every location, spotting which site is running smoothly and which needs attention.

2

Location detail view

A tappable card showing the Admin, Manager, and Accounting contact for that location - reachable by call or directions, the same view Managers and Accounting see for their own assigned sites.
