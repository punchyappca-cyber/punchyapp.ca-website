Remote Kiosk Hub

Total control over your physical attendance terminals. Remotely manage, monitor, and master your hardware deployment across the globe.

Zero-Touch Terminal Management

Stop dispatching IT staff for simple terminal resets. Punchy puts every physical kiosk in your organization just one click away from your central dashboard.

Sub-Location Precision

Don't just know which branch a kiosk is in—know exactly where it's located. Name your terminals "Main Lobby", "Back Dock", or "Breakroom B" for pinpoint tracking.

Instantly lock down terminals for maintenance or security reasons. Disabled kiosks show a professional, branded "Maintenance" screen to employees automatically.

Over-the-Air Sign Outs

Remotely force-sign-out all users or specific terminals to ensure security compliance during shift changes or emergency situations.
