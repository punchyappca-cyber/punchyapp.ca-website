Global Oversight

Global Multi-Location Governance

Command your entire business empire from a single interface. Punchy unifies decentralized locations into a coherent, high-visibility management framework.

Unified Control, Scoped Access

Managing ten locations is a challenge; managing five hundred is impossible without the right tools. Punchy provides the architectural backbone for massive scale.

Hierarchical Manager Scoping

Assign regional managers to specific clusters of locations. They see only the data they need, while top-level admins maintain a true "Global View" of the entire organization.

Instant Location Switching

No more multiple logins. Admins can hot-swap between different branch dashboards in real-time to review local schedules, punches, and terminal health.

Centralized Employee Profiles

Track employees as they move between locations. Unified profiles ensure training records, payroll details, and performance metrics stay with the individual, regardless of their current branch.
